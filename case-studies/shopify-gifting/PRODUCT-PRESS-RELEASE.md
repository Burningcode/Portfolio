## Headline

**Creator Commerce Platform introduces Shopify-enabled gifting so brands can broaden creator access and scale product seeding**

## Subheadline

Creators can apply for gifting collaborations in 3 clicks or respond to targeted invitations, while Shopify merchants can move completed gift orders into normal fulfillment without storefront code, spreadsheets, or repetitive coordination.

## Announcement

Creator Commerce Platform today announced Shopify-enabled Creator Gifting for brands of every size, from direct-to-consumer merchants to enterprise teams, that have outgrown messages and spreadsheets or want to spend less time coordinating a growing creator gifting program.

Brands can create a campaign, open it to applications or invite specific creators, and build a reusable gifting catalog. They choose eligible products, set the number of items or total gift value, and define campaign deadlines. Creators can apply with information about their audience and previous performance. If selected, they can choose the right size, color, and other variants and move an accepted gift directly into the merchant's Shopify fulfillment workflow.

## Why gifting matters now

Product gifting has become a measurable creator-marketing channel, but more volume does not automatically create more value. An [Influencer Marketing Hub analysis of product seeding](https://influencermarketinghub.com/product-seeding-strategy/) reports that 92% of marketers say product seeding increased brand awareness and 76% say it contributed to sales. The same analysis, citing Traackr research, says 61% of marketers report that fewer than half of gifted creators produce content.

That gap makes fit, completion, and measurement as important as fulfillment. Brands need to reach more relevant creators, reduce the effort required to send products, and see whether gifts lead to useful content and conversion.

## The customer problem

Brand marketing teams often allocate more inventory to creator gifting than they can distribute through a manual workflow. In the prior messaging experience, creators could request products that were not available for gifting. Teams then had to repair the mismatch, confirm sizes and variants, collect delivery information, enter orders, chase fulfillment status, and reconcile the resulting activity in separate spreadsheets.

That work limited many participating brands to roughly 5 to 15 gifting collaborations per month. It also narrowed creator access because teams could only invite as many people as they had time to coordinate. Creators outside the initial outreach had no efficient way to express interest, while brands faced more applications than they could consistently review when they opened a broader opportunity.

## The product experience

Shopify-enabled Creator Gifting supports both discovery and targeted outreach. A creator can find an open gifting campaign and answer questions about their audience and previous performance in 3 clicks. Brands can also invite specific creators directly.

As applications arrive, the creator commerce platform vets them against the campaign criteria and surfaces the strongest applicants for brand review. The brand keeps the final decision about creator fit. Approved applicants and invited creators then review the offer and choose one or more eligible products and variants within the brand's limits on product count and total gift value.

The creator can review and revise a valid product selection throughout the Shopify-hosted checkout. Browsing the assortment, confirming a selection, starting checkout, or submitting an application does not accept the collaboration. Acceptance occurs when the creator submits the complete order, and the interface explains that consequence before submission.

Shopify creates the initial order, identifies it as a gift, applies the brand's custom reporting tags, and sends it through the merchant's normal fulfillment process. The creator commerce platform brings the submitted acceptance, order reference, fulfillment state, recoverable exceptions, associated content, and performance results into the collaboration view. Accepting the gift authorizes fulfillment; it does not require the creator to publish content.

## How it works

1. **Connect Shopify:** An authorized brand administrator consents to the data sharing required for gifting and connects an eligible Shopify store.
2. **Create a campaign:** The brand defines the audience, application questions, campaign dates, and time-bound rules such as offer expiration.
3. **Configure gifting:** The brand builds an eligible gifting catalog, sets the number of products or total gift value, adds reporting tags, and can reuse the same configuration across multiple campaigns.
4. **Open access:** The brand opens the campaign to creator applications, invites selected creators, or uses both paths.
5. **Prioritize fit:** The platform uses campaign answers, audience data, and previous performance to surface top applicants. The brand makes the final selection.
6. **Select and accept:** An approved or invited creator confirms the allowed products, quantities, variants, and delivery information. The complete order submission records collaboration acceptance and sends the order to Shopify.
7. **Fulfill and measure:** Shopify creates the gift-tagged order and handles normal fulfillment. Brand and support teams can trace the collaboration, reconcile it with custom tags, and review the content and conversion that follow.

## Architecture and trust

The integration exchanges catalog, selection, order, and fulfillment data through Shopify APIs. Because the app adds no code to the merchant's storefront, it is designed not to affect storefront load time. Access remains limited to the minimum data and permissions required for the workflow, and a merchant must consent to that data sharing before connecting a store.

Shopify remains the authority for catalog, inventory, order, and fulfillment state. The creator commerce platform remains the authority for campaigns, creator applications and invitations, collaboration acceptance, and performance context. Shared identifiers and recoverable states let support teams trace an exception without rebuilding the collaboration from messages.

## Customer and business value

The product changes the unit of work. Brand marketing teams still make the decisions that require judgment, including campaign criteria, creator fit, eligible inventory, and whether the resulting content justifies the investment. The platform handles application triage and the catalog, selection, order, and fulfillment coordination that becomes difficult to operate at higher volume.

In an initial pilot with 5 enterprise brands, the Shopify integration worked inside existing operating workflows and helped the brands fulfill all available inventory allocated to gifting. Active brand-team handling time fell by approximately 95% per collaboration, and the brands supported approximately 10x the program scale of the earlier workflow.

Those figures describe approximate operating results from the pilot, not the performance of every direct-to-consumer or enterprise customer. Brand names, the exact measurement period, and the raw source of record are not included in this public portfolio case. The 95% figure refers to active handling time, not total elapsed time or feature coverage. The 10x figure compares program scale with the prior range of roughly 5 to 15 collaborations per month. Pricing, content output, and conversion were not isolated as causes of that change.

## Product principle

Automation should remove repetitive coordination without taking important judgment away from the customer. Broader access gives more creators a path to relevant gifting opportunities. Campaign criteria and platform prioritization help the brand focus that access. Shopify handles the fulfillment work, while the brand retains control over fit, inventory, economics, and the final creator decision.

More applications, accepted collaborations, and fulfilled gifts are not enough on their own. The product creates durable value only when the additional throughput produces more relevant creator content and that content drives conversion.

## Availability and platform direction

Shopify-enabled Creator Gifting is designed for eligible Shopify merchants, from direct-to-consumer brands to enterprise teams, that can consent to the data sharing required for the integration. It is not limited to an enterprise service tier.

Shopify is the first fulfillment wedge, not the limit of the product vision. Additional commerce and fulfillment integrations are planned so brands can use the same campaign and creator experience across more of their operating stack.
